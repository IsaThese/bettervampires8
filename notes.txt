Castlevania Dracula ability slots (vampire lord BV powers)
==========================================================

Lords assign each BV power to a Legends ability key. True Form swaps the
effect/name on paired combat slots (same power, different move).

Descriptions below match tihyo's Castlevania Dracula suit docs
(https://www.tihyo.com/characters/dracula-(castlevania)).

Combat slots
------------

Slot 1  —  bvVampireBite
  Humanoid : Vampiric Bite
    Bite the target, cause bleed, fill blood meter.
    ~6 damage; staggers ~5 seconds.
  True Form: Fury Swipes
    Claw flurry. ~10 damage + 2 bleeding.

Slot 2  —  bvHellfire
  Humanoid : Hellfire
    Three hellfireballs. ~15 damage + 2 burn.
  True Form: Wave of Fireballs
    Three large fireballs from the mouth. ~5 damage each; staggers.

Slot 3  —  bvBattlePrecognition
  Humanoid : Battle Precognition
    Anticipate attacks for ~10 seconds: voids most damage and ignores Distortion.
  True Form: Combat
    Melee mastery for ~15 seconds: damage ramps every few hits; can void basic
    melee; reduced enemy knockback after winded; skilled foes can block punches
    but not stagger.

Slot 4  —  bvDarkInferno
  Humanoid : Dark Inferno
    Hellfire variation — torrent of dark magical orbs.
    ~10 damage on contact (often ~20 total); stuns ~3 seconds.
    (Suit also has a rare “crit” variation when suit durability is low; BV
    lords call the normal TimedAbility path without that suit check.)
  True Form: Rampage
    Charge forward ~5 seconds, voiding damage and knocking things aside.
    ~5 damage per collision.

Slot 5  —  bvDraculaTeleport
  Humanoid : Teleport
    Engulf in flame/energy, vanish, reappear shortly after.
  True Form: Electric Wave
    Shockwave of electricity around you. ~25 damage to nearby foes.

Other Castlevania slots
-----------------------

Equip  —  bvTrueForm
  True Form
    Toggle Castlevania Dracula True Form morph. Unlocks the True Form versions
    of slots 1–5. Suit notes ~25% damage reduction while transformed.
    Grants Legends Transvection (levitate hover), not flight; clears in daylight
    outside sun-safe / vampire biomes.

Utility —  bvSoulSteal
  Soul Steal
    Drain nearby foes: ~5 damage twice, heal ~10 (up to ~20 with more targets).
    Chance to feed Soul Well essence.

Special —  bvDemonicMegiddo  (PowerType.special — special move slot only)
  Demonic Megiddo
    Ultimate AoE dome of chaotic magic. ~80 total damage in radius, staggers;
    you are invulnerable while it plays. Does not break blocks/terrain.

Special —  bvVampiresWrath  (PowerType.special — The Count)
  Vampire's Wrath
    Embrace / drain channel (same as The Count). Heavy damage while held;
    blood fill uses Horror wrath pulses with a BV floor of ≥30% on successful contact.

Armor  —  bvCape
  Cape
    Toggle cape mode (shroud / open cape visuals & related suit behavior).

Not a separate BV power
-----------------------
  Blood Flow Vision — Legacy vampire stats already drive PowersHandler.bloodVision.

Cooldowns (approx. suit ticks)
------------------------------
  Slot 1 / 2 : 200
  Slot 3     : 500
  Slot 4     : 400
  Slot 5     : 400
  Soul Steal : 500
  Megiddo    : 1000
  Cape       : 40
